IN DETAIL

Product Specifications

📦

Modular Post Pallet

A sturdy, robust system for rough use in high-traffic environments. Thousands of units are in use in cold, dry and outdoor storage across GCC countries.

  • Ideal for palletized cargo
  • Eliminates wooden pallets for loose cargo
  • Assembled and disassembled within seconds
  • No maintenance required
🏗️

Angle Frame Pallet — Type A & B

Rigid and stable with a 5-high stack and column loads up to 14 metric tons. Stack pads are engineered for strength and stability.

  • Type A: assembles within a minute; four heavy-angle posts lock into the base with 8 or 12 inter-locking members
  • Type B: for operations that don't need frequent disassembly
  • Option to replace wooden base with lighter metal base pallets
🚜

Metal Base Pallet

A lighter, stronger, highly durable and cheaper alternative to wooden or plastic pallets for internal warehouse use.

  • 2-way and 4-way forklift operation
  • Highly resistant to damage, reusable for years
  • Designed for use with Angle Frame Pallet
  • Customized designs available
🧰

Nestainers

The solution for logistics operations — standard boxed cargo or consolidation of non-standard packages, sacks, bales and baggage. Use indoors, outdoors, on wharfs, quays and onboard vessels.

  • Up to 10 MT per unit × 3 = 30 MT per column
  • High resistance to corrosion and rust
  • Easily stackable even in low-visibility conditions
  • Base design customizable to load size
🛢️

Drum Pallet

A practical, highly efficient solution for storage and movement of chemicals, oil and more. A base pallet with balance pads holds the drums in place.

  • Carries up to 12 drums in one forklift movement
  • Horizontal or vertical storage systems
  • Stores up to 24 drums in only 1.44 m²
🏭

Smartrack Fixed Racking

Engineered clearances for safe, efficient pallet handling.

  • Pallet overhang: 50 mm typical · Frame clearance: 100 mm min
  • Side clearance: 75 mm up to 6 m, 100 mm above
  • Aisle clearance: 100 mm typical · Transfer aisle type
  • Accessories: wall tie, pallet support, entry bar, container support, drum cradle, decking panel, wooden decking, column & frame guards
🚛

Smartrack Drive-in Racking

Ideal for block stacking of pallet loads that are otherwise unstable for self-stacking, and for numerous products of the same type.

  • Lift-off clearance: 100 mm up to 6 m; 50 mm above 6 m
  • Arm brackets with flancings and bolts for joint rigidity
  • Self-centering pallet supports & high-visibility upright guards
  • LIFO standard; FIFO possible with drive-thru racking
